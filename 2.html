Designed for movement, layering and modern exploration, The Captain Shirt translates the spirit of the open sea into a contemporary wardrobe piece.

The Captain Shirt is built around a relaxed, adventure-inspired silhouette. The design combines weathered character, structured detailing and a clean contemporary profile so it can work as a standalone statement or as part of a layered look.
Reinforced seams, carefully considered proportions and antique-inspired details create a piece designed to feel distinctive without becoming costume-like.
Wear it open over a simple base layer or button it for a sharper silhouette. Pair it with dark trousers, boots or relaxed denim for a complete Captain Abdullah look.
